Sources, trials & credits

This is a study reference, not a prescribing authority. Doses are for adults with normal renal and hepatic function unless stated. Always check your own institution's antibiogram and dosing guideline — local resistance patterns override every national figure here — and confirm doses against a current formulary before prescribing.

Key trials

TrialYearResultWhy it matters
MERINO2018ESBL bloodstream infection: 30-day mortality 12.3% pip-tazo vs 3.7% meropenem; failed non-inferiorityKilled the idea that pip-tazo is adequate for ESBL bacteraemia. Use a carbapenem.
STOP-IT2015N=518; a fixed 4-day course after adequate source control matched treating until physiologic normalisationEstablished short-course therapy for intra-abdominal infection — and that source control, not duration, is what cures it.
OVIVA2019N=1,054 bone/joint infection: failure at 1 year 13.2% oral vs 14.6% IV; catheter complications 1.0% vs 9.4%Ended the automatic "6 weeks of IV" reflex. The line is often riskier than the drug.
POET2019N=400 stabilised left-sided endocarditis: composite 9.0% partial-oral vs 12.1% IV; post-randomisation stay 3 vs 19 daysPartial oral therapy is possible in highly selected, stabilised endocarditis — an ID decision, not a floor decision.
SABATO2024Low-risk S. aureus bacteraemia: oral switch non-inferior (13% vs 12%) — but only 4.2% of screened patients were eligibleThe eligibility figure is the real lesson: almost no SAB patient qualifies.
Yahav · BALANCE2019 · 2025Uncomplicated gram-negative bacteraemia: 7 days non-inferior to 14. BALANCE N=3,608, 90-day mortality 14.5% vs 16.1%Seven days is the answer for stable, source-controlled gram-negative bacteraemia. Excludes S. aureus.
PTC (Dinh)2021CAP stable at day 3: 3 days of β-lactam non-inferior to 8 (cure 77% vs 68%)The evidence behind the 2026 ATS move to <5 days in stable, non-severe CAP.
ATS CAP guideline (Jones)2026Suggests <5 days (minimum 3) after stability for outpatient and non-severe inpatient CAP; strongly recommends ≥5 days for severe CAP. Reverses the 2019 steroid position — now suggests steroids in severe CAP and strongly recommends against them in non-severe CAPThe current CAP guideline, and the reason the durations in this guide are shorter than the 2019 numbers you may have been taught.
Talan · Daum2016 · 2017After I&D of a cutaneous abscess, TMP-SMX improved cure (80.5% vs 73.6%); clindamycin and TMP-SMX both beat placebo for small abscessesChanged abscess practice — drainage plus an oral antibiotic, not drainage alone.
ProHOSP2009PCT-guided therapy in LRTI: antibiotic exposure 5.7 vs 8.7 days, adverse effects 19.8% vs 28.1%, non-inferior outcomesThe trial that made procalcitonin credible.
ProACT2018N=1,656, 14 US hospitals: no difference in antibiotic-days (4.2 vs 4.3)The essential counterweight — PCT only removes excess duration, and only if clinicians act on it.
SAPS · PRORATA2016 · 2010ICU PCT algorithms: SAPS median duration 5 vs 7 days with lower 28-day mortality (20% vs 25%); PRORATA gained 2.7 antibiotic-free daysSupports the "<0.5 ng/mL or ≥80% drop from peak" stopping rule in the ICU.
ACORN2023Vancomycin + pip-tazo vs vancomycin + cefepime: no difference in AKI; cefepime had more neurological dysfunctionOverturned a practice-changing observational signal. A lesson in evidence quality.
CAPE-COD2023Hydrocortisone in severe CAP reduced 28-day mortalitySteroids now belong in the severe-CAP conversation.
de Gans2002Dexamethasone before/with the first antibiotic dose reduced unfavourable outcome and mortality in bacterial meningitisWhy steroids must precede antibiotics — the benefit is blunting the inflammatory burst from bacterial lysis.
PEN-FAST · PALACE2020 · 2023PEN-FAST <3 has a 96.3% NPV; PALACE showed direct oral challenge non-inferior to skin testing in those patientsTurns penicillin-allergy delabelling into something any clinician can start.
Parente2018MRSA nasal PCR for MRSA pneumonia: NPV 96.5% overall, 98.1% in CAPThe single easiest vancomycin de-escalation available on the wards.
Hepburn2004Uncomplicated cellulitis: 5 days non-inferior to 10 in patients improving at day 5Halved cellulitis courses.
AVOD · DIABOLO2012 · 2017Uncomplicated acute diverticulitis: antibiotics did not accelerate recovery or prevent complicationsMade observation without antibiotics a legitimate option in mild disease.
Kumar2006In septic shock, each hour of delay to effective antimicrobial therapy after hypotension onset was associated with a measurable fall in survivalThe origin of the one-hour antibiotic target in septic shock.
Halm1998Once clinical stability is reached, subsequent deterioration requiring escalation occurs in ≤1%The evidence base for the discharge vital-sign criteria.
MASCC (Klastersky)2000Score ≥21 identifies low-risk febrile neutropenia (PPV 91%)The basis for outpatient oral management of selected febrile neutropenia.
CURB-65 (Lim)2003A five-item bedside score stratifying 30-day pneumonia mortalityThe simplest CAP disposition tool; ATS/IDSA prefer PSI/PORT for the admit decision.
